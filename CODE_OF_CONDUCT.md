# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in InstaPortfolio respectful and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Include enough context to investigate; do not post private details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports and take appropriate action.
